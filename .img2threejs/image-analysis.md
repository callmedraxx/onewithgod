# Image analysis — ref/toilet.jpg (revision 2)

New reference: an isolated product render, three-quarter from front-left and
slightly above, evenly lit on white. Far better evidence than the showroom
photograph it replaces — the silhouette is unobstructed and no packaging
hides the seat.

## What changed from revision 1

The form is **not** the one previously modelled, and the difference is the
whole silhouette rather than a detail.

| | revision 1 (built) | revision 2 (this reference) |
|---|---|---|
| Bowl sides | straight linear taper | **concave — waisted** |
| Foot | full-width skirt to floor | narrow plinth, ~half the rim width |
| Cistern | short, square, untapered | taller, narrower, raked lower front |
| Seat | thin slab | **thick, soft-cornered, overhanging** |

## Layer 2 — Form & silhouette

- The bowl does not taper in a straight line. It sweeps **inward** from the
  rim, reaching its narrowest just above the floor, then flares slightly
  into a small rectangular plinth. Seen side-on the profile is concave,
  closer to a wine glass than a box.
- The cistern is a tall thin slab, roughly square in plan, whose lower front
  rakes back where it meets the bowl shelf.
- Symmetry is bilateral. Shape language is geometric with generous soft
  radii — squared, but nowhere sharp.

## Layer 3 — Decomposition

**Macro:** cistern → cistern lid (flat, slight lip, visible seam near the
top) → bowl body (concave) → plinth → seat → cover.

**Meso:** the seat and cover are notably thick and overhang the rim on all
sides; a clear parting line separates them. A shallow oval recess sits low
on the bowl's front face, where the trap runs.

**Micro:** a large soft specular sheet across the cistern's front face —
this is the read that tells you it is glazed rather than matte.

## Materials

Glossy white with a faintly cool cast; reflections carry a green-blue tint.
`color #f2f4f3`, `roughness ~0.07`, clearcoat over the body.

## Identity-defining features

1. **Concave, waisted bowl** narrowing to a small foot. This is the feature.
2. Narrow plinth at the floor — not a full-width skirt.
3. Tall, narrow cistern with a raked lower front.
4. Thick, soft-cornered seat and cover that overhang the rim.

## Occluded

Rear face, underside, waste outlet and bowl interior are not visible.
Reconstructed from category convention; approximate in those regions.
